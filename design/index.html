What changed in round 2

Desktop
  • Display size 75–100%, 80% by default. Everything fits at 1440×900.
  • Analysis presets (BRIEF / MAP / STORY), map size S/M/L.
  • Names never collapse: columns hide by priority, then the tower scrolls in its own panel.
  • INT back in the strategy view. TV MODE keeps your moment and delay.
Phone
  • App bar with session identity and switcher.
  • Session-aware tabs. Strategy, conditions and full race control are now on the phone.
  • Row tap opens the driver; follow is explicit; compare any two.
  • Playback and delay sheets, a bottom banner for moments, landscape layout.
TV
  • Toolbar and delay panel (custom M:SS, nudges, pause, GO LIVE); keys follow the same rules.
  • TIMING view brings back every old column. Team names in the tower.
  • No winner before the flag. Fills any screen shape; phone windows get the phone view.
Truth & motion
  • Everything is shown only once it is known (availability, not occurrence). The engine side is with Astra.
  • Inspect, then replay, then ↩ back. Spoiler-safe timeline.
  • Interrupted animations cancel cleanly. One reduced-motion policy. Coalesced at fast speeds.

Decisions and tradeoffs

DecisionWhyTradeoff
Denser desktop default (80%), not a flat 75% shrinkMatches the owner's feedback while keeping the smallest labels legible. A flat 75% takes them to about 7 px.Big monitors at a distance may want 90–100%; it's one setting.
Live pause grows the delay CONFIRMEDPausing your TV and the timing together keeps them in sync. It is the same delay concept, not a new mode.Live is no longer strictly "delay only". Capped at 5:00.
TV scales one composition CONFIRMEDOn a TV, readability comes from screen size and distance, not pixel count (the 10-foot convention). The grid still fills every aspect ratio. It is also the basis for a future standalone Android TV app: remote navigation, safe area and a session list are in place (tv.html?app=1).No bespoke 720p layout. Production must use tokens, not CSS zoom.
TIMING is a manual TV viewBrings back every old TV column without squeezing the default tower.Moment cards pause while it is open; the ticker continues.
Code + team in TV and phone rowsTeam identity without opening anything (reviewer and owner ask).Surname moves to TIMING and driver pages. A setting offers code + name.
Brand red stays in the markThe approved accent already matches the icon. Red also means LIVE, red flag and places lost.The icon's orange-yellow glow appears only in the mark and the opening.
Logic fixes delegated (WP-1)Astra found them and can prove them with Node. Claude kept the visual work.Copy such as "passes" under yellow stays until WP-1 lands.

Pit stopsTIMINGPit-lane transit only, never stationary time.
